# Planning when the decision is uncertain

Identify the consequential unknown: user need, information structure, interaction,
implementation constraint, or art direction. Develop approaches that differ in
mechanism and explain how each would serve the audience. Use a source read, small
prototype, reference, or task experiment to resolve the deciding uncertainty.

For consequential choices, an independently authorized reviewer can challenge the
assumptions. Give them the actual brief and evidence rather than a desired verdict.
Choose a direction based on the decisive evidence and strongest objection. Record
what would change the choice. Routine fixes need a direct plan, not an ensemble.
