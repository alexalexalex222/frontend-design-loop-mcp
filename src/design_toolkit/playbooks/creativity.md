# Purposeful design development

Use this when the brief calls for stronger identity, communication, or experience.
Ask which specific audience need the current treatment fails to serve. Find a
coherent direction in the actual content, references, brand, and use context.
Distinctiveness can come from composition, typography, imagery, content structure,
or interaction; familiarity can be the right choice for task clarity.

Assess the page as a whole. A section needs change when its treatment weakens the
intended experience, not because every section owes a novel signature. Propose a
mechanism and explain its benefit. Verify content claims; identify unsupported
claims as unverified unless evidence establishes falsehood. Preserve accessibility,
responsive usability, and the strongest existing elements. Capture the changed
states and keep the prior best source checkpoint if the experiment regresses.
